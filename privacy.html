Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tropical Braz Multi Service Corporation, doing business as Tropical Multi Service ("Tropical", "we", "us"), collects, uses and protects personal information. It applies to this website and to the operational communications we send, including SMS text messages and e-mail.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to run our operations and communicate with our team:

  • Contact information such as name, business e-mail address and mobile phone number, provided by employees, contractors and business partners.
  • Work information such as role, work schedule and service area, used to route operational alerts to the right person.
  • Messaging records such as the date, time and delivery status of messages we send, and replies received (for example STOP or HELP).
  • Website data: this website does not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

2. How we use information

  • To send operational alerts and notifications related to work assignments, such as emergency ticket alerts, escalations and shift handoff reports.
  • To coordinate dispatch, field operations and service quality.
  • To keep records required for auditing, compliance and service quality.
  • To respond to inquiries sent to us.

We do not use this information for marketing or advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

3. SMS text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are used only to deliver the messaging program described in our SMS Terms. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP. Message and data rates may apply.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate, such as our messaging provider (used to deliver SMS) and our e-mail and cloud providers, and only as needed to provide those services. These providers may not use the information for their own purposes. We may also disclose information when required by law.

5. Data security and retention

We protect personal information with reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including access restricted to authorized personnel. We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

6. Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or stop receiving SMS messages, by replying STOP to any message or by contacting us at the e-mail below.

7. Children's privacy

Our services and communications are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
