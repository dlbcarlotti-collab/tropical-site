Our services

We support telecom carriers from planning to construction, with trained field crews, dispatch coverage and the equipment to get the work done.

Underground Utility Locating

Locate and marking of buried telecom facilities in response to 811 tickets, including emergency tickets, with dispatch coverage and quality audits.

Fiber Construction

Construction of fiber optic networks for telecom carriers, including mainline, drops and splicing.

Fleet Rental

Vehicles and equipment for field operations, maintained and ready for utility and construction work.

About us

Tropical Braz Multi Service Corporation, doing business as Tropical Multi Service, is a Florida-based company headquartered in Deerfield Beach. Our team of field technicians, dispatchers and supervisors works to deliver safe, accurate and on-time services to the carriers we support.
