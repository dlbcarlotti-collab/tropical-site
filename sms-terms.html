Program description

Tropical Dispatch Alerts is an operational text messaging program operated by Tropical Braz Multi Service Corporation (dba Tropical Multi Service). It sends work-related alerts to our employees, field technicians, dispatchers, supervisors and managers, such as emergency locate ticket alerts, escalations when a ticket needs action, and shift handoff notices. The program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

Who receives messages and how consent is collected

Only Tropical team members who have agreed to receive operational alerts are enrolled. Consent is collected in writing (paper or electronic form) during onboarding or when a team member is assigned to a dispatch or field role. The form identifies the program, the type of messages, the expected frequency, and how to opt out. Consent is not a condition of employment, and team members may choose to receive alerts by e-mail instead.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with operational activity and the recipient's role and schedule, typically from a few messages per week to several per day during active emergencies.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile carrier plan. Tropical does not charge for these messages.

Opt-out

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages unless you re-enroll by replying START.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at dispatch@tropicalserv.com.

Sample messages

Tropical Dispatch: EMERGENCY ticket #266605472 (MARION) has no technician assigned for 6 min. Please check IRTH now. Reply STOP to opt out.

Tropical Dispatch: Ticket #266605472 still without response after 2h. Escalated to supervisor. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Carrier liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for details.
